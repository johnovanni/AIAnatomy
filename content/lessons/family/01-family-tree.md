The transformer is one member of a large family of programs that act intelligently. This lesson will open with a family tree you can explore. It has three main branches: programs written by a person, programs that learn from examples, and programs that learn from reward. Choosing a bubble will show what that kind of program is, what it is good at, what it is bad at, and a real-world example, with a button to open its lesson. Lessons not built yet will show as later. All of this is standard textbook material.
