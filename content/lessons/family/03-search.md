This lesson will let you play tic-tac-toe against a program that uses minimax search. It looks ahead through the possible replies, scores each outcome as a win, a draw or a loss, and picks the best move. A look-ahead tree will show the computer's options with their scores, and you will be able to open a branch to see the replies beneath it, together with a count of the positions it looked at. This is classic textbook material.
