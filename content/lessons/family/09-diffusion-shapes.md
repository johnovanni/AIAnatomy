This lesson will show a toy that learns to turn a cloud of random points into a shape, such as a spiral, one denoising step at a time, in the way described by Ho et al. (2020). You will pick a shape or draw your own, and train it again. Many image generators use the same method on pixels, or on compressed versions of them, as in Rombach et al. (2022).

*Sources: Ho et al. (2020) and Rombach et al. (2022).*
