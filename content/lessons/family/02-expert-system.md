This lesson will show an expert system that identifies animals by asking yes or no questions, such as whether it has feathers. You will be able to read its if-then rules, add, change or delete them, and follow a trace of which rules fired and why it reached its answer. You will also see how brittle hand-written rules are: an animal the rules never considered, such as a penguin or a bat, gets a wrong answer or none, and patching one rule can break another case. This is classic textbook material.
