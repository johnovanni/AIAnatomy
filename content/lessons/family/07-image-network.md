This lesson will let you draw a digit with the mouse and watch a small convolutional network, of the kind described by LeCun et al. (1998), read it. You will see its feature maps layer by layer and its probabilities for each digit from 0 to 9. You will also be able to train a fresh network yourself and watch its loss and test accuracy.

*Sources: LeCun et al. (1998).*
