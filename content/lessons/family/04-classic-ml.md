This lesson will let you place coloured points of two or three classes on a plot and watch two classic methods learn from them. A decision tree will draw its splits as lines and shaded regions, with a depth slider and its questions listed. Nearest neighbour will shade each region by the majority of the k nearest points, with a k slider. A deep tree, or k equal to 1, will show overfitting by carving round single odd points. This is standard textbook material.
