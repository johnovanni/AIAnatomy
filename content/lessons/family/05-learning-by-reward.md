This lesson will show learning by trial and error, with no examples of right answers, only rewards. You will edit the walls of a grid maze, and a Q-learning agent, as described by Sutton and Barto (2018), will explore it. You will see the value the agent gives each square and the arrow it currently prefers, and you will be able to change the rewards, the exploration rate and the discount.

*Sources: Sutton and Barto (2018).*
