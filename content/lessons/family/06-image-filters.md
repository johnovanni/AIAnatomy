This lesson will show one small 3 by 3 filter sliding over a digit that you draw or pick. You will be able to edit the filter's nine numbers or choose a preset such as a vertical edge, and see the feature map it produces. It will also compare the hand-made presets with the filters a small network learned, as in the convolutional networks of LeCun et al. (1998). The same few weights are reused at every position, an idea the transformer does not use.

*Sources: LeCun et al. (1998).*
