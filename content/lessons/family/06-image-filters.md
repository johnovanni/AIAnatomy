This lesson will show one small 3 by 3 filter sliding over a digit that you draw or pick. You will be able to edit the filter's nine numbers or choose a preset such as a vertical edge, and see the feature map it produces. It will also compare the hand-made presets with the filters a small network learned, as in the convolutional networks of LeCun et al. (1998). What is new in an image network is the small local window: a 3 by 3 filter looks only at a pixel's neighbours, and the same nine numbers slide across the whole image.

*Sources: LeCun et al. (1998).*
