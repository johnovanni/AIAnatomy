This lesson will look inside one denoising step of the toy, following Ho et al. (2020). You will see noise added to a shape step by step, and the schedule that controls it. You will see the model predict the noise in a noisy point, set against the true noise. A step slider will run the reverse process one step at a time, and fewer steps will show what quality is lost.

*Sources: Ho et al. (2020).*
